About & Motivation

With over 6,000 hours logged in Destiny 2, my passion lies deep within cooperative endgame architecture—specifically analyzing, dissecting, and understanding how raid and dungeon encounter mechanics, weapon synergies, and combat loops capture players for years.

I want to create the kind of unforgettable, community-defining moments and cooperative magic that Destiny provided for me and millions of others.
